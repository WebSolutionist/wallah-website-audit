## Approved design decisions

- Direction: Conversion Diagnostic Studio.
- Navy `#0A1931` is the dominant foundation.
- Teal `#00B4D8` is used as an intentional action and diagnostic signal.
- Use cards and grids with restraint, not as decoration.
- Use `headshot-removebg-preview.png` as the primary hero portrait.
- The hero image is integrated with an inline SVG diagnostic map.
- Use lightweight JavaScript for navigation, active section state, card expansion, reveal motion, and subtle pointer movement.
- Respect reduced motion preferences.
- Visitor facing copy uses no em dashes or dash punctuation.
